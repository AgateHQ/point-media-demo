## The Problem
A complete rethink of the media business and product model is needed to unlock a sector trapped between mass-market-hostile paywalls and volume-over-quality ad-supported models.

## The Opportunity: Reimagining the Mass Market
Despite an increasingly poor consumption experience, millions of people consume tabloid-style stories every day. With increasing pressure on the traditional business model, today's mass-audience products are dying… but their role is not.

There are no mass-market, paid-for, digital news products right now. That's not just a tragedy, it's a huge opportunity.

The need for compelling mainstream journalism has never been greater. We need new, mass-market, entertaining, trusted, habitual news sources.

**We need news that entertains. News with a big voice. News you can trust. News you look forward to.**

---

## The Point: The New Daily News Habit

**Accessible, entertaining and irreverent editorial.**  
+ **A coherent, compelling consumption experience.**  
= **An unmissable 10 minute daily news habit for millions of paying consumers.**

### Product Experience
- **6am daily**: Freely distributed summary of the daily news — a crafted piece of entertaining journalism that sets the day's agenda
- **Front page**: The big story of the day, linked from the email
- **Published once daily** with a cover price of 20p, frictionlessly paid via Axate when a reader moves beyond the home screen
- **Nothing gets in the way of the story** — the product experience is more 'Instagram stories' than 'news source'
- **~30 stories per day** — an entertaining mix, accessible and addictive. No sections, no fillers, just stories worth 10 minutes of your day
- **High-quality ads** complement the product experience; national awareness and location-aware advertising supported alongside each other
- **Advertisers can reward readers directly** via the Axate wallet and e-commerce integration
- **A sense of completeness drives habit**: readers feel they've 'read the news' again. Gamification, bonuses and rewards build habitual desire to 'finish' each edition
- **Network-first design**: consuming stories from around the network (and beyond) is encouraged throughout

## Growth from Diversity
Mass-market news will be followed by introducing new products in high-value segments:
- Sports
- Entertainment
- Celebrity gossip
- Lifestyle
- Business
- Opinion

Each new title extends the network's scope and customer relevance. New products can be launched quickly on a common platform with repeatable commercial mechanics.

Axate's platform will enable innovation at scale; a ready-made network for new products — created by us and others. Diversity and choice will drive growth across national, local and special interest audiences.

Individual creators can become brands on the network too, generating revenue from the content they create and share. Micro-nodes (like an integrated Substack) can generate substantial extra network revenue and allow talent to emerge and develop.

## The Power of the Network
We are building a completely new type of media platform: the first connected network of media products built to engage with, and drive direct revenue from, modern consumers — at scale.

Our network has the potential to unlock value across the entire population.

This network in turn will give birth to a new suite of quality media products, optimised to serve both mass and niche paying audiences and delivered on the same underlying network. These products are designed to drive new consumer habits and generate revenues from frictionless casual payments, quality advertising and commercial partnerships.

The network can support any number of national, local or specialist interest products and publishers — even those with opposing positions or temporary relevance — and benefit from the interplay between them.

Consumers pay for what they want, when they want it. No walls, no click-tricks. No doom-scroll. Instead, a growing ecosystem of targeted, high-quality experiences will serve discreet target audiences.

Building on Axate's proven casual payments platform, we will build a consumer revenue driven network with population-scale audiences.

### Revenue Streams (on publishers' own terms)
- Daily/weekly/monthly cover price
- Cross-network thresholds
- Incentivised AYCE (All You Can Eat) packages
- Network-specific ads and partnerships
- Embedded commerce partnerships
- Title-specific offers (e.g. local paid listings)
